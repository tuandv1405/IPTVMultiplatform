## Full description  *(limit 4000)*

```
TS IPTV is a player for the IPTV playlists you already have.

It ships with no channels. You bring the playlist — an M3U link from your
provider, an XSPF file, a JSON source, whatever you already use — and TS IPTV
turns it into a clean, fast, browsable TV experience on every screen you own.

WHAT IT DOES

• Imports M3U and M3U8 playlists, XSPF files, JSON sources and iptv-org style
  catalogues, from a URL or a file on your device.
• Reads XMLTV programme guides (EPG) so you can see what is on now and what is
  coming next, with correct times in any time zone.
• Groups your channels automatically using the group-title, tvg-group and
  category information already in your playlist.
• Remembers what you watched, so you can pick up where you left off.
• Keeps playing in the background with proper media controls on your lock
  screen and notification shade.
• Works offline for everything that does not need the network: your playlists,
  groups and guide data are stored on your device.

BUILT FOR EVERY SCREEN

TS IPTV is one Kotlin Multiplatform codebase running natively on Android, iOS,
Windows, macOS and Linux. The layout adapts from a phone in your hand to a
desktop monitor without feeling like a stretched phone app.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS

Your playlists, channels, programme guide and watch history live in a local
database on your device. We do not upload them, we do not index them, and we
cannot see them. Signing in is optional and only exists to sync your profile
across devices.

IMPORTANT: THIS APP PROVIDES NO CONTENT

TS IPTV does not bundle, host, index or rebroadcast any television channel. It
is a player, in the same way a PDF reader is not a publisher. You are
responsible for the sources you add and must be legally entitled to use them.
If you do not already have a playlist from a provider you subscribe to, this
app will show you an empty library — and that is by design.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

Playlists: M3U, M3U8, XSPF, JSON, iptv-org JSON
Guides: XMLTV (.xml and .xml.gz)
Streams: HLS, DASH, progressive HTTP, and anything else your platform's media
stack can decode

Questions, bugs, or a format we should support? Get in touch — the contact
address is on the listing and inside the app.
```

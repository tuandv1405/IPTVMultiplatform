# Release notes — TS IPTV 1.1.4, first production release

| Build | versionCode | Track | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tsptv.1.1.0004` | 1010004 | Production | First release to all users. The notes introduce the app as a whole, not the changes since the test builds |

How to use: in Play Console › **Test and release** › **Production** › create a release › **Release notes**,
paste the two blocks below together, including the language tags. Play splits them per language. Play allows
at most 500 characters per language (vi-VN 495, en-US 482).

Before pasting:
- Keep "gói Không quảng cáo" / "No ads plan" only if the `tsiptv_noads` subscription is active in Play
  Console. Otherwise delete "; gói Không quảng cáo" and "; No ads plan".
- Sending playlists to the TV and device sync need an account. There is no room to say so here; the listing
  and the app explain it.
- TS IPTV ships no channels or sources, so the notes must not suggest any.

The "first release" notes in `listing-en.md` and `listing-vi.md` are older and out of date (they still list
iOS and desktop and miss cast, sync, addons and TS IPTV Source). Use this file.

<vi-VN>
TS IPTV chính thức ra mắt: một trình phát cho mọi nguồn IPTV của bạn.

Điểm nổi bật:
• Chuyển video đang xem sang TS IPTV trên Android TV cùng Wi-Fi chỉ với 2 chạm
• Gửi danh sách phát lên TV, không phải gõ bằng remote
• Đồng bộ danh sách phát giữa các thiết bị
• TS IPTV Source: tự tạo nguồn với trang chủ, phim lẻ, phim bộ riêng
• Addon tương thích Stremio

Và: M3U kiểu Kodi (DRM), XSPF, JSON, .strm; lịch EPG; đổi luồng, phụ đề; phát nền; điện thoại, tablet, Android TV; gói Không quảng cáo.
</vi-VN>

<en-US>
TS IPTV is here: one player for all your IPTV sources.

Highlights:
• Move what you're watching to TS IPTV on your Android TV in two taps
• Send playlists to your TV, no typing with the remote
• Sync playlists across your devices
• TS IPTV Source: build your own source with a custom home, movies and series
• Stremio-compatible addons

Plus: Kodi-style M3U (DRM), XSPF, JSON, .strm; EPG guide; stream and subtitle switching; background play; phone, tablet, Android TV; No ads plan.

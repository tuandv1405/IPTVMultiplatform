Pair your phone and play on TV

Watch on your phone, then move the stream to the living-room TV in two taps, or send a playlist to the TV so you never type a long link with the remote. This guide shows how to pair a phone with the TS IPTV TV app once, then cast and send.

TS IPTV includes no channels. Casting moves what you are already playing, from playlists and sources you added, to your own TV. Nothing passes through our servers: the phone talks to the TV directly over your home network.

What you need

Supported
TV (receives)An Android TV or Google TV (TV, TV box or stick) with the TS IPTV app. The LG and Samsung TV apps cannot receive yet.
Phone (sends)An Android phone or tablet, or an iPhone, with TS IPTV. Desktop apps cannot send yet.
NetworkPhone and TV on the same home network. The TV may use Wi-Fi or a network cable; the phone uses Wi-Fi.
AccountNot needed to pair and play on TV. Sending a playlist needs you to be signed in on the phone.

Update both apps to the latest version first: the phone and the TV must speak the same version of the feature.

1. Get the TV ready

  1. Open TS IPTV on the TV.
  2. In the menu on the left, open Settings › TV & devices.
  3. The Receive from phone section says Ready to receive as “TV name” and shows an address such as Connect by IP: 192.168.1.20:47123. Note the address in case you need it later.

Stay on this screen while you pair: a new phone can pair only while TV & devices is open on the TV. This stops anyone else on the network from putting a pairing code over your film. Once a phone is paired, it can play on the TV whenever TS IPTV is open there, on any screen.

2. Pair the phone (once)

  1. On the phone, play any channel or video.
  2. Tap the TV icon at the top of the player. The Play on TV sheet opens and looks for TVs on your network.
  3. Your TV appears under Other TVs on this Wi-Fi. Tap it.
  4. The TV shows Pair a phone with a 6-digit code. Type it on the phone (Enter the 6-digit code shown on the TV).
  5. The TV says Paired with “phone name”, and the stream starts playing on the TV.

You can also start from the Home screen: tap the gear at the top › TV & devices.

iPhone: the first time, iOS asks whether TS IPTV may find devices on your local network. Choose Allow. If you chose “Don't Allow”, switch it on in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › TS IPTV.

The code is valid for 2 minutes. Three wrong codes end the attempt; after several failed attempts, pairing is locked for 10 minutes. The code is never sent over the network, so nobody can read it off your Wi-Fi.

3. Play on TV

  1. Make sure TS IPTV is open on the TV (any screen; the TV only listens while the app is in front).
  2. On the phone, play what you want to watch and tap the TV icon.
  3. Your paired TV is listed first under Your TVs: Paired · Ready means it answered. Tap it.
  4. The phone says Playing on TV name and the TV starts playing. Films and episodes continue from where you were on the phone.

The TV receives everything the stream needs to play, including any headers or DRM settings of your playlist, so a channel that plays on the phone plays on the TV the same way. Use the TV remote to pause, zap channels or stop.

Send a playlist to the TV

Typing a long playlist link with a remote is slow. Send it from the phone instead. You must be signed in on the phone.

  1. Open TS IPTV on the TV.
  2. On the phone, either:
    • Home › gear › Select Playlist, then the Send to TV icon next to a playlist; or
    • when adding a playlist, enter the link (or pick a file up to 1 MB), then tap Send to TV instead of importing it on the phone.
  3. Pick your TV. The phone says Sent. Confirm on the TV.
  4. The TV asks Receive the playlist “name” from phone? Choose Receive. The playlist is added and its channels load on the TV.

The free plan includes a few sends a day; the sheet shows how many are left, and you can earn extra ones with a short optional ad. The Unlimited plan removes the daily limit (fair use). Casting what you are watching (step 3) is never limited.

The TV doesn't show up: connect by IP address

Phones find TVs automatically, and if the network blocks automatic discovery the phone also checks every address of your home network. If the TV still does not appear:

  1. On the TV, open Settings › TV & devices and read the address next to Connect by IP, for example 192.168.1.20:47123.
  2. On the phone, in the Play on TV sheet, tap Connect by IP address, type the address exactly (with the :47123 part) and tap Connect.
  3. Pair with the code as usual. The phone remembers where the TV answered and tries there first next time.

If even this fails, the phone and the TV are not on the same network (see the table below).

Unpair a phone or forget a TV

  • On the TV: Settings › TV & devices › Paired phones › choose the phone › Unpair. That phone needs a new code before it can send again.
  • On the phone: Home › gear › TV & devices › under Your TVs, Forget this TV.

Unpair any phone you do not recognise.

Troubleshooting

You seeWhat to do
No TV foundCheck the list under For your TV to show up: same network (not a guest network, not mobile data), no VPN on either device, TS IPTV open on the TV. Then tap Search again, or connect by IP address.
Paired · Not found. Open TS IPTV on the TV.The TV app is closed or in the background, or the TV is asleep. Open TS IPTV on the TV and tap Search again.
To pair, open TV & devices on the TV, then try again.Pairing only works while that screen is open on the TV. Open Settings › TV & devices on the TV, then tap the TV again on the phone.
Wrong code / The code expiredType the code currently on the TV screen; a code lasts 2 minutes. Start again from the phone for a new code.
Too many failed attempts. Wait 10 minutes.Wait 10 minutes, then pair again.
The TV is pairing with another device / The TV is busySomeone else is pairing, or a code was just cancelled. Wait a minute and try again.
Phone and TV clocks differ. Check date and time.Turn on automatic date and time on both devices.
Couldn't reach the TV.The TV left the network or the app closed. Open TS IPTV on the TV and check both devices are on the same network.
Found by IP, never automaticallyYour router blocks discovery (multicast or mDNS), common on mesh systems and some ISP routers. Connecting by IP and paired TVs keep working; you can also turn off “AP isolation” or “client isolation” in the router settings.
Plays on the phone, not on the TVThe TV's player may not support that stream format, or the stream only allows one device or one network address at a time. Try another channel to check.
Hotel, office or campus Wi-FiThese networks usually stop devices from seeing each other. Casting needs a home network, or the phone's own hotspot with the TV connected to it.

Privacy and security

  • The phone and the TV talk directly over your local network. Stream links, headers and playlists never go through a TS IPTV server or the cloud.
  • Only phones you paired with the on-screen code can send to your TV. Every request is signed with a key made during pairing, and old or copied requests are rejected.
  • The TV listens only while TS IPTV is open on it, and accepts new phones only while TV & devices is open.
  • A received playlist is added only after you choose Receive on the TV.

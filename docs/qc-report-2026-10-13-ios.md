## Not done / known gaps (by design or needs accounts)

- **Push notifications:** iOS binds `NoPushPlatform`; needs an APNs key and FirebaseMessaging (`setup-push-ios.md`).
- **Ads, billing:** no AdMob or StoreKit on iOS by product decision (`prd-admob.md` §3, `prd-subscriptions.md`).
- **Google Sign-In:** not implemented on any mobile platform (`AuthRepositoryImpl.signInWithGoogle`); the plist now has the client ID and URL scheme it needs.
- **Subtitles / audio tracks on iOS:** `textTracks()` empty, as documented in `MediaPlayer`.
- **Picture in Picture on return:** standard iOS behaviour: the floating window stays when the app comes back; its restore button brings the video into the app. Android leaves PiP on return.
- **TLS:** Android's player trusts all certificates; AVPlayer does not, so self-signed HTTPS streams fail on iOS only.
- **Lock-screen controls for HLS:** left to `AVPlayerViewController` (`updatesNowPlayingInfoCenter`), not verified on the lock screen.
- **Test account:** `qa.ios.sim@tsiptv.test` was created in Firebase `tsiptv-8bdd6` for this pass. Delete it in Firebase Console → Authentication when no longer needed.
